06 · New Idea
Quick-pin parameters
Star any parameter to pin it to a highlighted section at the top of the inspector. For nodes with many settings, this brings your most-edited fields to the front so you never have to scroll past groups you don't touch.

How it works
Every parameter has a ☆ star icon on hover. Click it to pin the field to the highlighted section at the top. The pinned section uses a subtle category-color background to stay visually distinct from the normal params below.

Pinned params are saved per-node-type, so pinning "Model" on an AI Agent pins it for every AI Agent in every workflow.
